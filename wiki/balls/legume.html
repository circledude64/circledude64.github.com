A legume (IPA: /lɛˈguːm/ le-GOOM) is an invasive type of ball, usually found in kitchens around moldy and old foods.

Behavior

legumes feast on expired/moldy food and house inside walls and corners of buildings or homes. Their destructive behavior is part of why they need to be eliminated the moment they are spotted.
