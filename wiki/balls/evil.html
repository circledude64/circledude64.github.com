evil is a ball in BALL RACE game, and appears in speed's adventure 2.

Behavior

speed's adventure 2

evil is seen when speed encounters him before the portal.

Lore

evil has multiple abilities:

• Time travel - it can go back and front in time wherever it wants to, unavoidably affecting the present. Also has:
 • Snapshots - to revert to a state before evil's intervention in a timeline, and
 • Library - an endless hallway of rotary knobs, only evil has access to it. Its main purpose is to correct any inequalities (compared to the unmodified present) after affecting whichever timeline it's in.
• Reality jumping - Clear from the name.
• Shapeshifting - can transform into any shape.

Trivia

• evil was originally a community suggestion. It evolved past what the suggester originally intended.
