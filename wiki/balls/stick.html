stick

stick is a ball in BALL RACE game (third ball by ID) and speed's adventure.

Behavior

BALL RACE game

stick sticks to any surface it touches for a few frames, scaling more as hits are garnered.

speed's adventure

stick can be collected to replenish one life. They're mostly seen in adjustable's boss arena when the player's HP is below 3.

Trivia

• The name derives from its behavior of sticking to surfaces.
