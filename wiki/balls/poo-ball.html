POO BALL is the antagonist and boss in speed's adventure 2.

Behavior

speed's adventure 2

POO BALL is the only boss in the game, being fought in its arena.

POO BALL picks attacks at random:

• square toss - Summons squares resembling part of its nested-square design that bounce around the screen from the borders before disappearing into them.
• explosion - Explosions appear transparently around the player a second before exploding.
• bouncer - Large gradiented (brown-orange) squares bounce from the floor, moving from one side to the other. A bolt variant (brown-purple) bolts down instead and moves faster. Rare chance that both appear simultaneously.
• swipe - Crescents appear from the screen sides and dash to the other side at the player's height.
• pulse - Random numbers of crescents pulse out of POO BALL. Chance for individual crescents to point at the player.
• spin-up - POO BALL shoots a line of crescents while spinning up. Chance for individual crescents to point at the player.

These attacks can appear after crossing the 50 HP benchmark:

• Down - POO BALL moves down.
• quad shooter - Crescents shoot out in four directions around it while the pattern rotates.
• fake-out - Similar to above, but denser and rotates faster. Doesn't deal damage.
