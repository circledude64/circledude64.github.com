super balls are a subset of balls in BALL RACE game, being amplified versions of their normal counterparts.

Behavior

BALL RACE game

Each super ball behaves similarly, borrows from, or amplifies a mechanic from its normal counterpart.

• super speed - higher speed increase.
• super grow - bigger size increase.
• super stick - sticking time in frames increases linearly
• super gravit - linear increase in gravity
• super basic - duplicates itself on hit with a 2/3 chance.
• super bolt - similar to bolt
• super duplicate - spawns with 13 other clones.
• super accelerate - accelerates faster.
• super float - faster and ignores water.
• super boost - constant boost, air drag decreases.
• super random - spawns as a random ball.
• super tennis ball - rebound gets higher instead of lower.
• super swap - more acceleration.
• super player - moves to cursor faster.
• super multiple - spawns as almost all of the ball roster.
• super drone - moves to cursor faster.
• super omega - spawns a random ball every few hits.
• super balloon - moves to cursor faster.
• super photo - ignores wall and breakable collision.
• super paranoid - has no air drag, higher acceleration from cursor.
