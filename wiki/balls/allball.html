allball is a ball in BALL RACE game, the 27th by ID.

Behavior

BALL RACE game

allball, like fusion, has a modal containing the fusable ball roster. However, it's a checklist.

Its behavior in a race is determined by what was selected in the modal.

Trivia

• Selecting only 2 balls in the allball modal uses fusion's texture.
• The name "allball" was decided by a community poll.
