ball race game (in-game as BALL RACE game, colloquially shortened to brg) is a game made by circledude64 and the main game in the ball franchise. It is a simple physics-driven ball racing game in which players select balls and watch them "race" across maps to the goal.

Overview

Gameplay

Race preview
The game features a rather basic gameplay loop:
• The player selects 2 balls (with one allowing to put multiple in a race),
• then they press the "CONFIRM" button and select a map or paste a level code in, • and watch the balls "race" to the finish line

The point of the races is to see which of the selected balls reaches the goal and wins the race.
The player can also use WASD or the arrow keys to pan the camera in a race.

Maps

The maps are composed of walls, bouncers, breakables (which have varying health; balls always deal one damage per hit), water that slows balls down (with super float and steel being unaffected), lava (editor-only feature) that kills balls on contact, and secret goals.

Interfaces

Main menu

Main menu

The main menu features
• a PLAY button (leads to the ball selection menu)
• credits with balls being named
• achievements (9) window
• a throbber (what?) indicating that tracks are being downloaded
• and ball of the day

Ball selection menu

Balls
The ball selection menu offers 4 pages of buttons, arranged in a 9x3 manner (not all are occupied) with only an icon for the ball.

The first and second pages offer normal balls, the third has super balls, and the 4th has secret balls.

Race menu

The race menu has 3 buttons:
• exit button (another one appears once a ball wins the race)
• camera button (follows one of the selected balls, odd behavior with multiple balls on one side)
• and pause button.

Settings

The settings offer options to:
• have multiple of the same ball race
• unlock camera restriction on arena (always off when a custom map is imported)
• toggle music/ball sound effect
• have the ball texture rotate freely (always on for buff dagger and not buff dagger)
• FPS counter
• changing the background
Hieroglyphics
• changing what tracks play
• having the selected balls not reset when exiting and re-entering the ball selection menu
• weapons spinning around balls (that deal 1 damage to breakables)
• race modifiers
