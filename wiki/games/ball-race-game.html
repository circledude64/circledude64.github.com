ball race game (in-game as BALL RACE game, abbreviation: brg) is a game made by circledude64 and the main game in the ball franchise.

Gameplay

The game's gameplay loop is:
• The player selects balls,
• then presses "CONFIRM" and enter the map number, or paste a level code in,
• and observe the balls racing.

The goal of the races is to see which of the selected balls reaches the goal and wins.

Map elements

• Walls,
• Bouncers - they make balls that touch it bounce up,
• Breakables - they can sustain a few hits before breaking,
• Water slows down the balls' descent and horizontal speed (exceptions are super balls and steel),
• and secret goals.

Controls

WASD to move the camera.
