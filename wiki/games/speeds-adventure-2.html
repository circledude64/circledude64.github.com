speed's adventure 2 (seen in-game as speed's adventure) is the second of the two speed's adventure games, appearing on the 14th of August, 2026. It's the sequel to speed's adventure.

Story

accelerate (the game's protagonist despite the logo) is woken up by speed from a "weird dream" of dying under the ℙ𝕆𝕆 𝕡𝕣𝕠𝕡𝕙𝕖𝕔𝕪 (which states accelerate will fall under POO BALL). accelerate then asks what he was needed for, with speed responding to see evil's portal "thingamabob" before asking what he dreamt about. Then accelerate goes to meet evil and enters the portal after being "...probably" curious.

After exiting the first portal they end up in Poland and meet POO BALL, who is mad over ice cream costing "A BUCK". It then reveals its plan to sink Poland and then the world before escaping.

Gameplay

speed's adventure 2 plays like its predecessor (a platformer), with an added dashing ability that lunges you toward wherever the cursor is pointing, with its speed depending on the distance between the cursor and the player.

Progression is level-based, with the start and endpoints being portals, with the exception of the first level.

Level structure varies; each focuses similarly on the dash mechanic, with the fourth level having a section crossable only with the dash.

Dream

As stated above, this level is an exception, as it's entered directly after pressing "play". In this level, the player's speed is halved, while jump height and gravity are lowered.

Death

Depleting all 3 (or 5 if in the boss fight) lives moves the player back to the spawn position. In the bossfight the player is dragged to the middle of the arena before POO BALL mocks them and regenerates some of its HP.

Boss Fight

After entering the arena, POO BALL introduces them to the Warsaw National Stadium and says today is going to be "AN VERY INTERESTING" match. accelerate complains about what they got into before POO BALL interrupts them and replies with "you irritate me" "𝑫𝑰𝑬" before the boss fight begins.

Mechanics

main article: POO BALL

After draining POO BALL's HP to 0, everything turns black except the player and POO BALL. It then tries to bargain a deal:
• They stop fighting,
• it'll only sink Poland under the condition that accelerate be in it,
• and the world will be untouched.

It also states that doing nothing is a refusal, "otherwise you accept". After the player does nothing (which is the only option), POO BALL gets mad and transforms into a second phase form before telling the player they're ready "TO TEAR YOU DOWN TO SHRIMPS-", only to be interrupted by something they hear.
An "oh" is spoken before a flashbang grounds it, leaving POO BALL as a semicircle, before fading away.
