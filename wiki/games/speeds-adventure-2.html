speed's adventure 2 (seen in-game as speed's adventure) is the second of the two speed's adventure games, appearing on the 14th of August, 2026. It's the sequel to speed's adventure.

Story

accelerate (the game's protagonist despite the logo) is woken up by speed from a "weird dream" of dying under the ℙ𝕆𝕆 𝕡𝕣𝕠𝕡𝕙𝕖𝕔𝕪 (which states accelerate will fall under POO BALL). accelerate then asks what he was needed for, with speed responding to see evil's portal "thingamabob" before asking what he dreamt about. Then accelerate goes to meet evil and enters the portal after being "...probably" curious.

After exiting the first portal they end up in Poland and meet POO BALL, who is mad over ice cream costing "A BUCK". It then reveals its plan to sink Poland and then the world before escaping.
