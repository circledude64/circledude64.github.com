speed's adventure 2 (seen in-game as speed's adventure) is the second of the two speed's adventure games, appearing on the 14th of August, 2026. It's the sequel to speed's adventure.

Story

accelerate (the game's protagonist despite the logo) is woken up by speed from a "weird dream" of dying under the ℙ𝕆𝕆 𝕡𝕣𝕠𝕡𝕙𝕖𝕔𝕪 (which states accelerate will fall under POO BALL). accelerate then asks what he was needed for, with speed responding to see evil's portal "thingamabob" before asking what he dreamt about. Then accelerate goes to meet evil

Gameplay

speed's adventure 2 plays like its predecessor (a platformer), with an added dashing ability that lunges you toward wherever the cursor is pointing, with its speed depending on the distance between the cursor and the player.

Progression is level-based, with the start and endpoints being portals, with the exception of the first level.

Level structure varies; each focuses similarly on the dash mechanic, with the fourth level having a section crossable only with the dash.

Dream

As stated above, this level is an exception, as it's entered directly after pressing "play". In this level, the player's speed is halved, while jump height and gravity are lowered.

Death

Depleting all 3 (or 5 if in the boss fight) lives moves the player back to the spawn position. In the bossfight the player is dragged to the middle of the arena before POO BALL mocks them.

Boss Fight

After entering the arena, POO BALL introduces them to the Warsaw National Stadium and says today is going to be "AN VERY INTERESTING" match. accelerate complains about what they got into before POO BALL interrupts them and replies with "you irritate me" "𝑫𝑰𝑬" before the boss fight begins.

Mechanics

POO BALL picks attacks at random:

• square toss - Summons squares resembling part of its nested-square design that bounce around the screen from the borders before disappearing into them. • explosion - Explosions appear transparently around the player a second before exploding. • bouncer - Large gradiented (brown-orange) squares bounce from the floor, moving from one side to the other. A bolt variant (brown-purple) bolts down instead and moves faster. Rare chance that both appear simultaneously. • swipe - Crescents appear from the screen sides and dash to the other side at the player's height. • pulse - Random numbers of crescents pulse out of POO BALL. Chance for individual crescents to point at the player. • spin-up - POO BALL shoots a line of crescents while spinning up. Chance for individual crescents to point at the player.

These attacks can appear after crossing the 50 HP benchmark:

• Down - POO BALL moves down. • quad shooter - Crescents shoot out in four directions around it while the pattern rotates. • fake-out - Similar to above, but denser and rotates faster. Doesn't deal damage.
