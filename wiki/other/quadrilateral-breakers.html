Quadrilateral Breakers (shortened to QB) is a game developed by LeftQuadrilateral.

Gameplay

• The player selects 2 balls from categories of:
• Block breakers damage blocks on contact (the largest category)
• Weaponballs damage blocks with their weapons instead
• Shooters shoot bullets at the blocks
• Then they press the "Confirm" button to start the fight, which consists of the selected balls breaking through 3 blocks to reach the finish line below.

Settings

The settings offer numerous things:
• An "Enter Code" button to enter codes for secret balls in
• block health presets (6) alongside the ability to manually change them
• a button to change how many balls appear on each side (left, right) of the arena
• an "Experiments" button with the only option being interpolation
• and a "Debug mode" button that unlocks Null

The balls, once selected, can also be adjusted for:
• Speed (speed multiplier, default 1)
• Size (default 100)
• Gravity (gravity multiplier, default -5)
