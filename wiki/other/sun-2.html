SUN 2™ is speed's adventure 2's secret boss.

Boss Fight

Encounter

main article: Hallo Bruder
SUN 2™ introduces accelerate to "THE TEST" while ignoring accelerate's questions. Thereafter it starts counting down before the "fight" starts.

Mechanics

SUN 2™ is a hit-based boss, i.e. your goal is to avoid its attacks and take the fewest hits.
• Spinners - it throws spinners in an arc, clockwise or counterclockwise.
• Lasers - transparent lasers appear before turning opaque and dealing a hit. Can predict where the player will be
• Swipers - horizontal diamonds appear from the top of the screen and move down, bouncing off of the side borders.
• Rays - these appear around SUN 2™ and bolt downwards. Chance to spawn above the player.
• Descending bars - have an opening in 3 different widths, go from top to bottom.
• Spikes - up to 3 can appear simultaneously, transparently, before turning opaque and retracting into the bottom. Chance for individuals to spawn below the player.
• Bomb - if the player stands still, a bomb appears over it. On explosion it deals a hit.

After completing the test, SUN 2™ evaluates it and considers it as "GOOD ENOUGH" before revealing the prize.

Trivia

• SUN 2™ originated as a Gartic Phone scribble.
