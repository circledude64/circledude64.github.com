The Hallo Bruder orb is an orb located in level 2 of speed's adventure 2.

Function

The Hallo Bruder orb indicates a safe area to fall into in an otherwise dangerous border. Falling there leads to the secret boss.

Relation

It's unknown whether the Hallo Bruder orb is related to any other ball.
