theories

ball race game has a lot of unexplained phenomena and inexplicable observations, mainly documented by the pseudonymous "The Journalist" in his journal, On Ball Race Game.

What does adjustable know?

adjustable is known around the community for that "IT KNOWS", but nobody is sure what it knows, or if it even does know. The Journalist's observations hint at adjustable knowing more than it should.

Some of The Journalist's observations (and their results) concern properties and features of adjustable no other ball would've or should've known about. How these were obtained isn't mentioned in His journal.

adjustable was also found to have several unexplained connections to the game's continuity, none of which He can explain.

sine, code, thrust and raytrace have been GONEd some time ago. No one knows who or why.
