Ballium is a material which exists in each living ball, in simpler terms, "what makes a ball a ball".

Mixes of different ballium types can exist.

Types

Ballium

Ballium is technically the default ballium variant.

Found in balls like speed and random.

Ballium-△

Ballium-△ has the ability to manipulate its ball's gravity.

Found in balls like gravit, float and honkber.

Ballium-□

Ballium-□ allows for higher-level velocity manipulation.

Found in balls like accelerate, boost or eunch.

Ballium-μ (Ballium-meta)

Ballium-μ possesses the ability to interact with the game or its environment itself.

Present in balls like photo or steel.

Ballium-▽

Ballium-▽ allows manipulating the size of oneself.

Present in grow and shrink.

Ballium-☆

Ballium-☆ is likely the most powerful of all ballium variants. It can create and modify other balls' contents (ballium). It isn't known whether it can delete ballium, though it was theorised to be able to.

Found in balls such as transform, duplicate or super basic.

Ballium-?

Ballium-? is a catch-all term for balls whose ballium composition is yet to be known or is too complicated to study for reasons.
