The Ball Chronicles

work in progress
The Ball Chronicles are a collection of myths and stories concerning the founding of the BRG world and its inhabitants. Verifiability and legitimacy/accuracy unknown.

The following list of stories was copied from what was found and translated, edited.

Adjustable

[...] As the Balls gather in the center for a sacrifice; the Sacrifice of a pig. The pig is taken to the podium by St. Gomer [and] [...]1 As it progressed, the day got darker, the Balls indifferent [likely them thinking it's about to rain]. Then, people started noticing green in the sky. At first, people assumed it was Caulethare's signal for the First [easter season]; Too late they realised. Green thunder started appearing out of the sky, so did the Balls' fear.
Suddenly blinded by a strike, a new among them appears: A halo fluctuating between blues and greens, unique to itself; Same size as the Balls, unlike any other deity. [...] Before long, it goes toward [St.] Gomer, confronts it: "Were you aware of what you did, of your bads?" "I swear to Thou, such pig was evily given to me; I was incautious" replied Gomer in anguish.
[...] Unseen before, Adjustable starts lifting St. Gomer a few feet. [...] Then it and Gomer start flying into the [now clear] skies; Unbeknownst to them, this would be the last time they saw Gomer.
